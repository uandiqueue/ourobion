# profile_signature_flower

Asset type: Flutter Profile identity accent

Primary request: Create a premium hyperrealistic signature flower motif for the Profile tab: one graceful pale-yellow anemone-like bloom, two small buds, a few naturally irregular pale-green leaves, fine branching stems, ivory porcelain collars, and tiny champagne-brass nodes.
Reference direction: Match the UI seed's calm botanical-luxury mood, realistic petals, high-key material treatment, porcelain-white mechanics, and restrained brass. The result must feel personal and distinct from the accepted camellia spray; it is not a logo, crest, or emblem.

Style/medium: Hyperrealistic premium botanical product cutout.
Composition/framing: Compact asymmetric diagonal sprig with generous clear blue padding on every side. Keep every petal, leaf, stem, and mechanical detail fully inside the canvas and clearly separated from the key.
Lighting/mood: Soft high-key object lighting confined to the subject; calm, refined, delicate.
Color palette: Pale yellow and warm white petals, pale green leaves, ivory porcelain, champagne brass. Do not use blue or blue-violet anywhere in the subject.
Materials/textures: Natural petal veining, realistic leaves, fine stems, smooth ceramic collars, minute polished brass nodes.
Constraints: One isolated connected motif; crisp clean silhouette; no contact shadow; no text; no watermark; no logo; no rectangular white box.
Avoid: camellia, peony bouquet, baby-breath filler clusters, symmetrical badge, logo mark, dark sci-fi, neon, horror, heavy machinery, clutter, cartoon, anime, low-poly.
