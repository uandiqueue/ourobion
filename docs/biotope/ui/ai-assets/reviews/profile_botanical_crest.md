## UI Seed Fit

The asset fits the seed mood while expanding the palette: real-looking white,
blush, and muted mauve flowers are arranged around a restrained ivory porcelain
and brass node. It reads as a botanical crest without copying or embedding the
Biotope logo.

## Background Mode

Correct. The asset has transparent corners and no rectangular white box.

## Slightly Off

The crest is denser than the smaller profile accents, so it should be used at a
larger size or with enough whitespace around it.

## Flutter Usage

Use as a profile badge-style artwork, account milestone decoration, or identity
panel accent.
