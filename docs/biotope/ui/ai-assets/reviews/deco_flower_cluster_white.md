porcelain joint, and tiny brass connectors match the seed's delicate card
accents. The botanical elements remain dominant and readable at small scale.

## Background Mode

Correct. The final is a 1254 x 1254 `Format32bppArgb` PNG with all four corner
alpha values at 0. The audit found 1,250,180 transparent pixels, 8,936 partial
edge pixels, 313,400 opaque pixels, zero blue-fringe pixels, and inset bounds of
`(180, 133)` to `(1041, 1125)`.

remains secondary to the flowers and leaves at intended card-corner scale.

## Flutter Usage
