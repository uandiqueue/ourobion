# empty_scan_bloom

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: Flutter Scan empty-state illustration
Background mode: transparent final PNG after chroma-key removal.
Primary request: Create a quiet premium scan-readiness bloom: one small realistic white orchid-like flower with two buds, a short curved stem, one pale-green leaf, and a subtle open ivory porcelain sensor ring with three tiny champagne-brass markers.
Reference direction: Match the UI seed's calm high-key biomechanical botanical system. The flower remains natural and primary; the sensor ring should suggest scan readiness without becoming a HUD or logo.
Scene/backdrop: Place the isolated subject on one perfectly flat, uniform solid pure-blue `#0000FF` chroma-key background for later removal. The blue must reach every canvas edge and corner with no white area, gradient, texture, floor plane, vignette, reflection, cast shadow, or lighting variation.
Style/medium: Hyperrealistic premium botanical product cutout.
Composition/framing: Compact centered asymmetric object with generous clear blue padding. Keep the full flower, buds, leaf, stem, and open sensor ring safely inside the canvas.
Lighting/mood: Soft high-key lighting confined to the subject; calm, expectant, uncluttered.
Color palette: Warm white petals, pale green, ivory porcelain, restrained champagne brass. Do not use blue or blue-violet in the subject.
Materials/textures: Real petals and leaf veins, fine stem, satin porcelain ring, minute brass markers.
Constraints: One connected empty-state motif; clean silhouette; no contact shadow; no text; no watermark; no logo; no rectangular white box.
Avoid: medical scanner, neon HUD, complete circular badge, target reticle, heavy machinery, dark sci-fi, horror, gore, cartoon, anime.

## Attempt 2 Refinement

Attempt 1 made the porcelain scan ring too large and badge-like. Attempt 2
reduced it to a thin partial crescent behind the lower stem, limited the scan
cue to a small fraction of the composition, and made the orchid, buds, stem,
and leaf the dominant read. Full rings, near-complete circles, targets, badges,
emblems, and logo-like framing remained explicitly prohibited.
