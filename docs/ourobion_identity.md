# Ourobion — Brand Identity

*Master brand and product identities. This document covers the core **Ourobion** identity and its two established products, **Ourobion Nao** and **Ourobion Biotope**.*

---

## At a glance

Ourobion is a life-science company built around a single conviction: that human, animal, and environmental health are one connected, living system — and that our understanding of it is never finished.

The brand is a **branded house**. There is one master identity — an open ouroboros drawn as a living cell — and every product wears it. Products don't get separate logos; they share the master's ring and serpent and change only the **nucleus** at the centre. The constant ring says *Ourobion*; the centre says *which* Ourobion.

| Product | What it is | For | Centre | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ourobion** | The master brand | Everyone | Nucleus (cell) | Teal — clinical meets biological |
| **Ourobion Nao** | The health-centric graphical brain that powers Biotope | Biotope maintainers / operators | Knowledge graph | Dark sci-fi — teal → violet |
| **Ourobion Biotope** | The user-facing smart-health super-app | Health-focused end users | Biomechanical bloom | Warm gold on white |

# Part I — Ourobion (master brand)

## Concept & name

**Ourobion** fuses two ideas:

- the **ouroboros** — the ancient serpent that swallows its own tail, a symbol of cycles, renewal, and wholeness; and
- **bion** — from the Greek *bíos*, the fundamental unit of life: the cell.

The mark is built as a **living cell**. A single serpent coils around a circular axis, weaving over and under itself like a strand of DNA, its head reaching back toward its tail. At the centre sits a **nucleus**. The result reads at once as a coiled serpent (the myth) and a cell (the biology) — a fitting emblem for work that spans the connected systems of human, animal, and environmental health.

## Core belief — the open loop

Unlike the classic ouroboros, **Ourobion's loop is deliberately left open.** The serpent never closes on itself; a gap remains between head and tail, and the ring is a near-circle rather than a sealed one.

This openness *is* the brand. The traditional ouroboros is a closed, self-contained cycle — eternal repetition. Ourobion breaks that closure on purpose, because **in biology the loop of understanding is never finished.** There is always new knowledge, always more to learn. The gap is also a refusal to think *inside* the loop, or inside a box: a system that reaches beyond the cycle rather than circling within it. It reframes the ouroboros from a closed loop of repetition into an **open spiral of discovery.**

## Design principles

**One continuous strand.** The serpent is a single unbroken line — the thread of life and knowledge running through the whole mark.

**The open loop, by design.** The unclosed gap is the brand's central idea: endless learning, thinking beyond the loop.

**Structure meets process.** The flat ring is a stable *axis* — the framework; it never twists. The serpent is the *living process* — dynamic, coiling, weaving through it. Structure and life, held together.

**Meaning encoded in the geometry.** The ring is divided into **23 segments**, and the serpent makes **23 crossings** as it weaves over and under — a quiet reference to the 23 chromosomes of the human haploid set. The number is meaning, not ornament.

**The cell.** A nucleus at the centre, a coiling boundary around it — the fundamental unit of life, and the literal root of the name.

**Geometric and reproducible.** Every part derives from circles and a parametric helix. It scales cleanly from favicon to billboard and can be regenerated exactly.

**Harmony through gradient.** Depth and vitality come from a graded family within a single hue range — never a clashing accent.

## Anatomy

- **Ring axis** — a flat circular band cut into 23 radial segments, bent into a loop left open at the seam. The stable framework.
- **The serpent** — one continuous tapered body coiling helically around the ring, 11.5 turns = 23 crossings, passing alternately in front of and behind the ring so the weave reads as a DNA double-helix.
- **Head toward tail — left open** — the head curls toward the tail across a gap that never closes.
- **Nucleus** — a solid core inside a thin envelope ring at the centre, completing the reading of a cell. In the products, this nucleus is the slot that changes.

## Colour & symbolism

Teal is the meeting point of blue and green — it carries blue's associations (trust, depth, clarity, clinical credibility) and green's (life, growth, health) without committing to over-used medical blue or generic "eco" green. It also reads as bioluminescence and the cool glow of fluorescence microscopy, tying the palette to the science of the cell.

The mark is not a single teal but a **graded family sweeping from deep teal through cyan to blue** across the 23 segments — giving the sense of a living, shifting organism, and reading as a spectrum: many shades, one family, mirroring "one health" (many domains, one connected system).

## Typography

The wordmark **ourobion** is set in **Outfit** — a geometric sans — at a light weight (200), lowercase, with generous letter-spacing. Lowercase keeps the brand approachable; the geometric letterforms echo the circular geometry of the mark; the light weight signals precision and calm. Outfit is open-source (Google Fonts); in production files the wordmark is outlined to paths, so no font is required to render the logo.

## The feature system

Products are built by keeping the **ring and serpent constant** (the master identity) and **swapping the nucleus** for a per-product glyph, framed by the same envelope "membrane." The parent uses the solid nucleus; products use their own centre glyph, kept in one consistent construction so the family always reads as one brand.

Products are named in an **"ourobion [name]"** lockup — the parent set as a small tracked kicker above the product name — never as separate logos. A product earns a distinct centre glyph and palette; it never leaves the ring.

# Part II — Ourobion Nao

## What it is

**Nao** is the reasoning layer — a health-centric graphical brain that ingests, connects, and orchestrates the signals flowing through Biotope. It is the product the **Biotope maintainers** work with: the operators tending the system, not the end users living in it.

## The name

From the Japanese *nō* (脳), "brain." Nao is the intelligence that powers everything downstream.

## The mark — a knowledge graph

The nucleus becomes a **mind-map / knowledge graph**: a bright central hub node, four mid nodes radiating out on connecting edges, each branching again to smaller leaf nodes. It reads as *structured* intelligence — a brain modelled as a network of related concepts, the orchestration core that coordinates the system. Drawn as light nodes and thin edges, so it reads as precise infrastructure.

## Colour — dark tech, sci-fi

Nao takes the master brand's **dark register**: the coil runs bright teal → blue → violet on a near-black ground — the glow of instruments, dashboards, and operator screens. Teal anchors it to the Ourobion core; the drift toward blue and violet signals reasoning, depth, and machine intelligence. **Dark is primary** because Nao is infrastructure; a light variant is provided for light UIs and print.

# Part III — Ourobion Biotope

## What it is

**Biotope** is the user-facing smart-health super-app — the environment in which a person's health is tended and flourishes. It is what the **health-focused end user** lives in day to day.

## The name

A *biotope* is an area with uniform environmental conditions that supports a particular community of life. For an app that treats the body as a living system to be cultivated — *"your biological system is blooming"* — it names the environment in which health flourishes.

## The mark — a biomechanical bloom

The nucleus becomes a **flower built to machine tolerances**: eight pointed petals, each with a central vein, tipped with a small mechanical fastener dot; at the core, a precision ring with eight tick-marks and a solid centre. Organic form, engineered construction — life and technology fused. It is the emblem of a *cultivated* system: nature, but instrumented, measured, and cared for — a neofuturistic biomechanical botanical garden.

## Colour — warm gold

Where the master brand is teal, Biotope shifts to a **warm gold** key. Gold evokes warmth, vitality, cultivation, and light — a living garden in golden hour, and a premium, calm, human feel appropriate for a consumer health companion. The petals and coil are graded across a gold ramp (warm gold → deep bronze), the same "graded family, never flat" principle in a warm register. **Light is primary** — gold on pure white, the app's signature look; a dark variant brightens the gold for dark mode.

# Part IV — The family together

## Hierarchy

The products tell a clear story from infrastructure to experience:

**Nao → Biotope → the user.**
Nao is the brain (infrastructure, for operators). Biotope is the body it powers (the app, for users). Read together, the family maps a living system: intelligence at the core, an environment around it, a person at the centre of care.

## System logic

- **One brand, one logo.** The ring and serpent are constant across every product — they are the identity.
- **The centre carries the meaning.** Each product changes only the nucleus glyph and its palette; everything else is shared.
- **Palette signals register.** Teal/violet for infrastructure (Nao), warm gold for the consumer app (Biotope), always a graded family, never a flat fill or a clashing accent.
- **Naming is consistent.** Every product is an "ourobion [name]" lockup, the parent kicker above the product name; products are never spun off into standalone logos.

## Extending the system

New products join by earning a centre glyph and a palette, drawn in the same construction language, and wearing the same ring. A product should get a distinct centre only when it is a genuinely standalone capability with its own audience; smaller features take descriptive labels rather than marks. A true sub-brand with more independence is reserved only for a product that grows into a flagship of its own.
