Ourobion

Patient safety does not rely on one perfect check. It stacks imperfect ones so their holes do not
line up — James Reason's Swiss-cheese model. We built our research pipeline the same way: of eleven
health relationships drafted from the literature, one was held back from people.

The README at github.com/uandiqueue/ourobion is the front door: judge credentials, references, and
an appendix carrying corpus counts, per-verdict tables, model results, and the prior-work boundary.


Problem

Health apps collect well and explain badly. After days of logging, people are shown their own
numbers again. Ourobion began with signals those apps overlook — gut and urinary health. A health
company hires people to find patterns and read the research behind them. We are three builders;
unable to hire that department, we grew one.

The obvious fix — attach a language model, ask it why — is the most dangerous thing to put in front
of a health claim: a model will state a correlation as a cause, reverse which came first, and stretch
a mouse study onto a person. The problem is not data or model access. It is grounding the
interpretation, when the best tool for it is the least trustworthy part of the system.

Existing approaches fall short: trusting the model assumes the answer. Asking the same model
"are you sure?" inherits its blind spots. Retrieval with citations still lets it misread the paper it
cited. Manual evidence grading — GRADE, Cochrane reviews — is rigorous but costs experts months per
question.

Before building, we fixed one criterion: no research claim reaches a person unless it points back
to its paper and survives review by a model that did not write it.


Approach

biotope is the phone app: quick daily logging, patterns in a person's own data. nao is the research
workbench, where every proposed relationship can be opened and inspected. We enter the Agentic
Systems track: the research loop is judged; biotope is where it must land.

Why an agent rather than one well-prompted call? A single call asks one model to find, interpret, and
check its own evidence — one slice of cheese. Our loop stacks layers that fail differently: papers
retrieved, a relationship drafted, its quotes checked by deterministic code, fresh evidence retrieved
again for a reviewer at another company, a numeric serving gate, and a language check before anything
reaches a person. Any layer can stop the run.

OpenAI's GPT-5 drafts the relationships from open-access papers; Agnes AI's Agnes 2.5 Flash retrieves
its own evidence and reviews them. The router refuses to start if writer and reviewer come from the
same company, because a model checking its own family's work shares its blind spots. That enforced
separation, not call volume, is why Agnes is here; it is also Singapore-built, fitting an ASEAN
product.

Models are used only where interpretation is needed. Deterministic code handles identity, quotation
checks, budgets, routing, non-diagnostic language, and release — layers that must behave identically
every run.


Evidence

The corpus holds full text for 911 papers. From a narrow slice, GPT-5 drafted eleven relationships;
Agnes returned one supported and ten partial. Ten cleared the serving gate; one did not. That whole
loop ran unattended in the cloud, dispatched from nao, three times end to end. Clearing the gate
makes a relationship eligible, not visible — it reaches someone only when it matches that person's
own data. Two have.

Eleven is small, deliberately: depth on a curated slice, not breadth. In nao, any one opens
onto the drafted claim, its quoted spans, the citation, the verdict, and the decision that followed.

We trained two specialist checkpoints. Viceroy classifies causal versus correlational wording;
it scored 0.866 where a keyword-matching baseline scored 0.507 (macro-F1).
Zebra classifies whether evidence supports a claim; it missed its pre-registered bar of 0.70, so the
code refuses to promote it. Neither serves the product. Both disagreed substantially with Anthropic's
Claude Haiku 4.5 on 96 real papers — but without adjudicated truth, that is disagreement, not
accuracy. A system that can say "not proven" is evidence too.


Constraints

The independence rule is close to free at our volume — Agnes carries the token-heaviest stage at no
cost. It is not free in time: verification adds a second model call with its own retrieval. If the reviewer is unavailable, verification stops rather
than falling back to the writer.

Retrieval cost is paid once at ingestion and amortised over every read, under budget stops. nao runs
on a Cloudflare Worker with a CPU limit, so long runs go to a scheduled job.

The GPU container we requested from GMI Cloud did not arrive in time, so both models trained locally
on Apple Silicon. Personal health rows stay isolated; automated checks keep user-facing language
non-diagnostic.


Honesty & Trajectory

biotope shows research-backed cards today on our seeded demo account. A judge signing up fresh sees
an empty app — by design: it needs your logs before it has anything of yours to say. Better said
than found later.

Every layer has holes. "Verified" means only that a claim faithfully reads the paper it cites, not
that the wider literature agrees. Ten relationships are eligible to be shown; two have surfaced, and
we have not traced why the other eight have not. Zebra failed its own bar. The
Claude comparison has no ground truth. The sample is eleven relationships on a few metric pairs.

With two more weeks: run the single-call baseline comparison, adjudicate the disagreement set with a
second labeller, record the per-edge trace showing which relationships surface and which do not, and
split papers into sections with sentence-level identifiers so drafting quotes precisely.

Further out, corroboration: when several papers independently support the same relationship,
agreement becomes a reliability score rather than a new claim — beyond our model-call budget today.

The reviewer never sees a health fact. It sees a claim, sources, and an instruction to refute them —
readable in the schema. Swap the corpus and metric list and the same gate runs elsewhere.
