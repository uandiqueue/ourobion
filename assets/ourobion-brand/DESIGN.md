# Ourobion — Brand & Logo Design Principles

*Version 1.0 — design documentation for the Ourobion identity.*

---

## 1. Concept

Ourobion fuses two ideas:

- the **ouroboros** — the ancient serpent that swallows its own tail, a symbol of eternal cycles, renewal, and wholeness; and
- **bion** — from the Greek *bíos*, the fundamental unit of life: the cell.

The name and the mark express one idea: **life as a continuous, self-renewing system.**

The logo is constructed as a **living cell**. A single serpent coils around a circular axis, weaving over and under itself like a strand of DNA, its head reaching back toward its tail. But — unlike the classic ouroboros — **the loop is deliberately left open.** The serpent never closes on itself; a gap remains. At the centre sits a **nucleus**.

That opening is the heart of the identity. The traditional ouroboros is a *closed* cycle — eternal, self-contained, endlessly repeating. Ourobion's serpent breaks that closure on purpose, because **in biology the loop of understanding is never finished.** There is always new knowledge, always more to learn — an open-ended, endless inquiry. The gap is also a refusal to think *inside* the loop, or inside a box: it is a system that reaches beyond the cycle rather than circling within it.

The result reads at once as a coiled serpent (the myth, reimagined) and as a cell (the biology) — a fitting emblem for work that spans the connected systems of human, animal, and environmental health.

---

## 2. Design principles

**One continuous strand.** The serpent is a single, unbroken line — the thread of life and knowledge running through the whole mark. Nothing in it is a loose element.

**The open loop — by design.** Where the classic ouroboros closes on itself in an eternal, self-contained cycle, Ourobion's serpent stops short: a gap is left between head and tail, and the ring is a near-circle rather than a sealed one. This openness *is* the brand's core belief — that biological knowledge is never finished, that learning is endless, and that the system thinks *outside* the loop rather than circling within it (or inside a box). It reframes the ouroboros from a closed cycle of repetition into an open spiral of discovery: renewal that moves forward.

**Structure meets process.** The flat ring is a stable *axis*: it lies in the plane, it does not twist, it is the framework. The serpent is the *living process*: dynamic, coiling, weaving through it. The mark holds structure and life together in one form.

**Meaning encoded in the geometry.** The ring is divided into **23 segments**, and the serpent makes **23 crossings** as it weaves over and under — a quiet reference to the 23 chromosomes of the human haploid set. The number is meaning, not ornament.

**The cell.** A nucleus at the centre, a coiling boundary around it — the fundamental unit of life, and the literal root of the name.

**Geometric and reproducible.** Every part derives from circles and a parametric helix. There are no hand-drawn flourishes; the mark scales cleanly from a favicon to a billboard and can be regenerated exactly.

**Harmony through gradient.** Depth and vitality come from a graded family within a single hue range — never from a clashing accent. The mark feels alive without feeling busy.

---

## 3. Anatomy of the mark

- **Ring axis** — a flat circular band, cut into **23 radial segments**. It is the helix's axis bent into a loop that is deliberately left open at the seam; it never twists. It represents the stable framework of the living system.
- **The serpent** — one continuous tapered body that coils helically around the ring, completing **11.5 turns = 23 crossings**. It passes alternately **in front of** and **behind** the ring; that over/under weave is what reads as a DNA double-helix.
- **Head toward tail — left open** — the serpent's head curls *toward* its tail across a small gap that never closes. The opening is intentional: the loop of life and knowledge stays unfinished, with room always left for the next discovery.
- **Nucleus** — a solid core inside a thin envelope ring at the centre. It anchors the composition and completes the reading of a cell.

## 4. Colour system

### Why teal

Teal is the meeting point of blue and green. It carries blue's associations — trust, depth, clarity, clinical credibility — and green's — life, growth, health — without committing to either the over-used medical blue or the generic "eco" green. It also reads as **bioluminescence** and the cool glow of fluorescence microscopy, tying the palette directly to the science of the cell.

### Why a gradient, not one colour

The mark is not a single teal but a **graded family that sweeps from deep teal through cyan to blue.** This is deliberate:

- It gives the mark depth and the quality of a *living, shifting* organism rather than a static badge.
- It reads as a **spectrum — many shades, one family** — mirroring the idea of one connected system made of many domains, and the diversity of life expressed from one shared code.

A single flat colour looked lifeless; a warm accent (we trialled coral) broke the harmony. The graded teal→blue keeps everything in one family while still giving each segment its own identity.

### Symbolism of the steps

| Tone | Role in the mark | Association |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep teal** | the ring, the nucleus core | depth, stability, the genetic anchor |
| **Cyan / aqua** | the brightest mid-tones of the coil | vitality, energy, the living glow |
| **Blue** | the cool end, toward the serpent's head | intelligence, trust, clinical & technical credibility |
| **The teal → blue sweep** | the coil winding from tail to head | growth and continuity — the unbroken spectrum of life |

## 5. Typography

The wordmark **ourobion** is set in **Outfit** — a geometric sans-serif — at a **light weight (200)**, **lowercase**, with generous **letter-spacing**.

- *Lowercase* keeps the brand approachable and human, not institutional.
- The *geometric* letterforms echo the circular geometry of the mark.
- The *light weight* and open tracking signal precision, calm, and modernity.

Outfit is open-source (available via Google Fonts), so it is safe to embed across web and product surfaces.

## 6. Usage

**Clear space.** Keep a margin of at least the nucleus diameter around the full mark; never crowd it with other elements.

**Minimum size.** The full graded mark reads well down to roughly **32 px**. Below that the segments and the weave begin to muddy — use the provided simplified favicon instead.

**Light vs. dark.** Use the light palette on white or pale backgrounds; use the dark palette only on `#0B1D24` or darker. Don't place the light mark on dark or vice versa.

**Do**
- use the supplied files and palette as-is;
- keep the gradient inside the teal–blue family;
- preserve the mark's proportions and the head-to-tail orientation.

**Don't**
- recolour outside the family (no warm/coral accents);
- stretch, skew, or rotate the mark;
- detach the nucleus from the coil, or recolour parts individually;
- add drop shadows, outer glows, or other effects beyond the provided dark treatment.
