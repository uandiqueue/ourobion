# Ourobion Nao — Logo & Design Notes

*The mark for **Ourobion Nao**, the health-centric graphical brain that powers Biotope.*

---

## Where it sits

Nao is one product in the Ourobion family. Every product shares the same **master identity — the open ouroboros ring and its coiling serpent** — and changes only the **nucleus** at the centre. That constant ring is what says "Ourobion"; the centre says *which* Ourobion. Nao's centre is a **knowledge graph**.

(For the master brand system, the open-loop symbolism, and the ring/serpent construction, see the main Ourobion brand documentation.)

## The name

**Nao** — from the Japanese *nō* (脳), "brain." Nao is the reasoning layer: a health-centric graphical brain that ingests, connects, and orchestrates the signals flowing through Biotope. It is the product the **Biotope maintainers** work with — the operators tending the system rather than the end users living in it.

## The mark — a knowledge graph

The centre is a **mind-map / knowledge graph**: a single bright **hub node** at the core, four **mid nodes** radiating out on connecting edges, each branching again to smaller **leaf nodes**. It reads as intelligence that is *structured* — a brain modelled as a network of related concepts, the orchestration core that coordinates everything downstream. Like the master mark, it is drawn as light nodes and thin edges (no heavy fills) so it reads as precise infrastructure.

## Colour — dark tech, sci-fi

Where Biotope is warm gold, Nao takes the master brand's **dark, sci-fi register**:

- The coil runs **bright teal → blue → violet** on a near-black ground — the glow of instruments, dashboards, and night-shift operator screens.
- **Teal** anchors Nao to the Ourobion core; the drift toward **blue and violet** signals reasoning, depth, and machine intelligence.
- **Dark is primary** because Nao is *infrastructure* — it lives on technical surfaces. A **light** variant (teal → blue on white) is provided for light UIs, print, and documentation.

## Typography

The lockup stacks the parent over the product: **ourobion** as a small tracked kicker (Outfit 300) above **nao** (Outfit 200). Outfit is open-source; in the supplied files the wordmark is outlined to paths, so no font is required to render the logo.

## Usage

Use **dark on `#0B1D24` or darker** (primary), and **light on white/pale**. Keep clear space of at least the envelope-ring diameter around the mark. The full graph holds together to ~40 px; below that use the supplied **favicon** (a simplified hub-and-nodes glyph). Keep the coil within the teal→violet family; don't recolour individual nodes.
