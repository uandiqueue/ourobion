small brass pin feel curated and herbarium-like without using paper, labels,
fake text, decay, or a heavy sepia treatment.

## Background Mode

Correct. The final is a 1122 x 1402 `Format32bppArgb` PNG with all corner alpha
values at 0. The audit found 1,433,642 transparent pixels, 5,781 partial edge
pixels, 133,621 opaque pixels, zero blue-fringe pixels, and inset bounds of
`(220, 85)` to `(834, 1323)`.
