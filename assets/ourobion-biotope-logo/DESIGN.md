# Ourobion Biotope — Logo & Design Notes

*The mark for **Ourobion Biotope**, the user-facing smart-health super-app.*

---

## Where it sits

Biotope is one product in the Ourobion family. Every product shares the same **master identity — the open ouroboros ring and its coiling serpent** — and changes only the **nucleus** at the centre. That constant ring is what says "Ourobion"; the centre says *which* Ourobion. Biotope's centre is a **biomechanical bloom**.

(For the master brand system, the open-loop symbolism, and the ring/serpent construction, see the main Ourobion brand documentation.)

## The name

A *biotope* is an area with uniform environmental conditions that supports a particular community of life. For a personal-health super-app that treats the body as a living system to be tended — *"your biological system is blooming"* — it's an apt name: the app is the environment in which a person's health flourishes.

## The mark — a biomechanical bloom

The centre is a flower built to machine tolerances: **eight pointed petals**, each with a central vein, tipped with a small **mechanical fastener dot**; at the core, a **precision ring with eight tick-marks** and a solid centre. Organic form, engineered construction — life and technology fused. It is the emblem of a *cultivated* system: nature, but instrumented, measured, and cared for. This mirrors the app's aesthetic — a neofuturistic biomechanical botanical garden, delicate white blooms wrapped around precise machinery.

Like the master mark, the interior is **stroke-based and open** (no heavy fills) so it reads as precise and botanical rather than solid.

## Colour — warm gold

Where the master brand is teal (clinical blue meets biological green), Biotope shifts to a **warm gold** key:

- **Gold / bronze** evokes warmth, vitality, cultivation and light — a living garden in golden hour, and a premium, calm, human feel appropriate for a consumer health companion.
- The eight petals and the coil are graded across a **gold ramp** (warm gold → deep bronze), the same "graded family, never flat" principle as the master mark, in a warm register.
- **Light is primary**: gold on pure white — the app's signature look. **Dark** brightens the gold for dark-mode surfaces on a warm near-black.

## Typography

The lockup stacks the parent over the product: **ourobion** as a small tracked kicker (Outfit 300) above **biotope** (Outfit 200), set in warm brown-gold. Outfit is open-source; in the supplied files the wordmark is outlined to paths, so no font is required to render the logo.

## Usage

Use **light on white/pale**, **dark on `#17130D` or darker**. Keep clear space of at least the envelope-ring diameter around the mark. The full bloom holds together to ~40 px; below that use the supplied **favicon** (a simplified, solid-petal bloom). Keep the gold family intact — don't recolour individual petals or introduce off-family accents.
